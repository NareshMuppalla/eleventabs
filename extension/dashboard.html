Welcome to Tab Butler 👋

Here is what it does from now on:

  • Sorts tabs into colour groups (Mail, Work, Dev, AI, Jobs, Social…) as you open them.
  • Sleeps tabs you haven't looked at for 30 minutes, so Chrome uses less memory. They come back when you click them.
  • Closes duplicate tabs, saves sessions, tidies bookmarks (copies only, nothing deleted) and cleans junk history.

Your saved passwords and your own tab groups are never touched.

⚙️ Change categories 📊 Go to dashboard

Tip: pin the extension (puzzle icon → pin) so the Tab Butler button is always one click away.
