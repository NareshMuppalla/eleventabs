Privacy policy

The short version: ElevenTabs collects nothing and sends nothing.

  • No servers, accounts, analytics, tracking or ads. The extension's security policy (connect-src 'none') blocks every network request from its own pages.
  • No access to web pages. There are no content scripts and no host permissions, so it can't read page content, forms, passwords or cookies.
  • What it reads: tab addresses and titles, to group, sleep and de-duplicate tabs. It reads history and bookmarks only after you allow those optional permissions, and only to show statistics, build a sorted copy of your bookmarks, or delete history entries you choose.
  • What it stores, locally in your Chrome profile: your settings, sessions you save, and minutes spent per site name. Full addresses and page titles are never stored for statistics. Sites on your "private sites" list are never timed.
  • Site icons come from Chrome's own local favicon cache, not from the internet.
  • Deleting your data: use Privacy & security → Erase ElevenTabs data, or remove the extension. Your tabs, bookmarks and history are not affected.

Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub.
