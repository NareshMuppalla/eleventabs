Privacy policy

Short version: Tab Butler collects nothing.

  • Tab Butler has no server, no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • Your settings, saved sessions and time-per-site statistics are stored only in your browser's local extension storage on your computer. They are deleted when you remove the extension.
  • It reads tab addresses and titles to group, sleep and de-duplicate tabs. It reads your history and bookmarks only to show statistics, to create a sorted copy of your bookmarks, and to delete history entries when you ask it to.
  • It never reads page content, passwords, cookies or form data, and never sends any data anywhere.
  • The dashboard shows small site icons loaded from Google's public favicon service (only the site's domain name is sent, as with any favicon).

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
