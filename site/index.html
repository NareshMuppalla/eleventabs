Security

Private by design,
not by promise.

Built so it is safe on a work profile. Your saved pages never leave your browser.

No network access

A strict security policy blocks every network request from ElevenTabs pages. There is nowhere for data to go.

Can't read web pages

No content scripts and no site permissions. It never sees page text, forms, passwords or cookies.

Permissions only when used

Importing Chrome bookmarks asks you first, and you can turn it off again at any time. Saving needs no extra permission.

Your data, your delete button

Everything is stored in this Chrome profile. Export it any time, or erase it in one click. Your Chrome bookmarks are never changed.

Open GitHub
Does it change my Chrome bookmarks?

No. Saved is its own library. Importing makes a copy. If you switch on "Also add to Chrome bookmarks", new saves are added to a separate ElevenTabs folder, and nothing you already have is moved or deleted.

How do I see all my bookmarks in one place?

Open the dashboard, go to Bookmarks and press "Bring in my bookmarks". Chrome asks once for permission. Turn on Auto-add and every bookmark you make in Chrome afterwards appears there too, in the right category.

How does it choose a folder?

By the site address, using rules you can edit: github.com is Dev, claude.ai is AI, naukri.com is Jobs. Anything it doesn't recognise stays in Unsorted until you decide. You can add your own folders and tell them which sites to catch.

Can I use it in my work Chrome profile?

Yes. It can't read pages and has no network access, so work content can't leave your computer. Each profile keeps its own data. If your company manages Chrome, IT may block extensions installed this way.

Will it close my tabs or lose my work?

No. Sorting only moves tabs into groups. A sleeping tab stays in place and reloads when you click it. Tabs are only closed when you press "Close duplicates".

Is it free? How do I remove it?

It's free. Open chrome://extensions and click Remove. Your tabs, bookmarks and history stay as they are.
